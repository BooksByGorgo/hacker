# way of the hax0r

**Gorgo, Queen of Sparta's** free, evolving books that help students develop hacker skills.

Hacking means exploring the limits of what is possible in a spirit of playful cleverness.
The series takes you from n00b to console punk to hacker & modder to guru to 1337 hax0r, one book at a time, building understanding and independent problem-solving skills layer by layer.
